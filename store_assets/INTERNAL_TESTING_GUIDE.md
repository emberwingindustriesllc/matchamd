# Google Play Console — Internal Testing Setup Guide

This guide details how to set up the **Internal Testing Track** on Google Play Console for your ~10 initial testers before launching the 14-day Closed Testing track.

---

## 🌟 Why Start with Internal Testing?

| Feature | Internal Testing Track | Closed Testing Track |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Audience Limit** | Up to 100 internal testers | Minimum 20 testers required |
| **Availability** | **Instant** (within minutes of upload) | Requires Google policy review (1-3 days) |
| **14-Day Mandatory Rule** | No 14-day restriction | **Mandatory 14 days continuous testing** |
| **Ideal For** | Early feedback with your 10 testers | Fulfilling Google's production requirements |

---

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Instructions to Invite Your ~10 Testers

### Step 1: Access Google Play Console
1. Log in to [Google Play Console](https://play.google.com/console) under **Emberwing Industries LLC**.
2. Select your app **MatchaMD** (or click **Create App** if you haven't created the app listing yet).

### Step 2: Set Up Internal Testing Track
1. On the left navigation menu under **Testing**, click **Internal testing**.
2. Click **Create new release**.
3. Drag & drop the signed release bundle:
   `android/app/build/outputs/bundle/release/app-release.aab`
4. Add Release Notes (e.g., *"MatchaMD Initial Beta Build"*).
5. Click **Save** and then **Next**.

### Step 3: Add Tester Email List
1. Click on the **Testers** tab inside the **Internal testing** page.
2. Under **Email lists**, click **Create email list**.
3. Name the list (e.g., `MatchaMD Core Testers`).
4. Type or paste the email addresses of your ~10 internal testers.
5. Click **Save changes**.

### Step 4: Share the Opt-In Link
1. Copy the **Join on Android** or **Join on the Web** link generated at the bottom of the Testers tab.
2. Send this link to your ~10 testers. Once they click the link and accept the invite, they can install **MatchaMD** directly from the Google Play Store on their Android devices!

---

## ⏭️ Next Step: Transitioning to Closed Testing Track
Once your 10 internal testers verify the app is working smoothly, we will promote the build to the **Closed testing track** and invite 20 testers for the mandatory 14-day countdown required for public release!
